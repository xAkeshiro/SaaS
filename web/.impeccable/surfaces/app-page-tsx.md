# Surface: marketing site (/, /pricing, /manifesto, /teams)

## Scope and mode
Persuade. The landing page is the lead surface; pricing, manifesto and teams inherit its world. Routes and anchors in PRODUCT.md stay fixed.

## Audience, job, action
A young adult avoiding one specific conversation arrives from a short clip or a campus link, usually on a phone at night. They must believe the other person will really push back and that they will leave with the exact words to say, then join the waitlist. Teams visitors (career centers) must find the pilot request in one click.

## Proof available
Problem statistics with sources, the scenario library, the persona and coach prompts, the working text demo. No users, testimonials, logos or outcomes exist; every transcript and debrief shown is labeled as an example.

## Constraints
Name is open (Unmute is a working name; keep it swappable). Keep the owner's Cluely reference: product leads the first viewport, fast, polished, moving. Zero em-dashes. No eyebrows, no metric strips, no feature-card grids.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment
The steamed bathroom mirror people rehearse in, where the mirror talks back. Memorable moment: the fogged arched mirror in the first viewport, where the other person's line wipes through the steam at speaking pace, your reply follows, an amber post-it lands with the sentence to say next time, and the visitor can wipe the fog with a pointer or finger.

## Unresolved
Final product name. Exact glaze hue is tuned on screen against contrast targets. A voice (not text) demo waits for the app.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The site is the steamed mirror people rehearse in, and this mirror talks back. It refuses the category default: a floating app window on a pastel mesh, a stat strip, and rows of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: One continuous wall of glossy deep-green subway tile in running bond, with handmade tone variation and pale grout. Everything is an object mounted on that wall: arched mirrors in brushed-chrome bezels with live condensation, frosted-glass panels for reading, amber post-it notes for every debrief and dare. Amber is vanity light and paper, never paint. Mona Sans carries all type across its weight and width axes; a marker hand appears only on post-its. Phosphor icons.

STORY: The visitor watches the other person push back, sees a debrief hand over the exact sentence, tries a rehearsal themselves, and joins the waitlist believing this is practice with a real opponent that wants them to need it less.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left seven columns on the tile: the headline in two lines, heavy and slightly condensed; a subline of twenty words; the email field with an amber "Get early access" pill; a quiet "Try a rehearsal" link. Right five columns: a tall arched mirror, fogged. The roommate rehearsal wipes through the steam line by line at speaking pace; an amber post-it lands with the next sentence. Moving the pointer or a finger across the glass wipes the fog, which slowly returns.

FORM: The steamed bathroom mirror, candidate 7 of 7 on the resonance-ordered list; seed key 732f7891 (degraded roll: roll service unreachable, no challengers).

RAISE, from the top-ranked short-form captions candidate: speech appears at speaking pace, word by word, never as a finished block.

TRANSLATION of the owner's Cluely reference: Cluely's light product window becomes the luminous mirror, so the product still leads the first viewport, in this world's glass.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
