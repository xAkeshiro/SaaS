How it works

Rehearse. Debrief. Repeat. Then do it for real.

1 · Rehearse

The other person talks back.

Pick the conversation or describe your own. The AI plays the other side with a voice, a mood and a personality. It interrupts, sighs, goes quiet, pushes back. Kind, neutral or hostile.

2 · Debrief

Exactly what to change.

What worked, where you folded, how long you took to get to the ask, filler words, and the two sentences to try next time. It remembers your patterns: "you apologize before every ask."

3 · Daily rep

Three minutes, every day.

A streak and a scenario picked from what you have coming up. Connect your calendar and "interview Thursday" becomes Tuesday's rep.

4 · Real mode

Warm up, then make the call.

A 60-second warmup and a cue card before the real thing, a debrief after. Unmute never listens to real calls. Phones do not allow it, and you would not want it to.

Not a companion

It exists to make you need it less.

AI companions are built to keep you talking to them. Unmute is built to get you talking to people. Every rehearsal ends with a real conversation to go have. Every persona is clearly synthetic. No romantic roleplay, no endless chat, no "I missed you."

  • A coach, not therapy. Crisis language routes to real resources, every time.
  • Practice rooms. A friend plays the recruiter while the AI coaches. The AI plays the panel while your friends rate you.
  • Dares. "Call the pizza place and negotiate a discount." Shareable clips, voice-changed if you want.
  • The before-and-after. Your first call next to your tenth. That clip is the whole pitch.
