On campus

Students sign more fine print than anyone.

First leases. Student deals that quietly convert to full price. Textbooks billed to tuition unless you opt out by a buried deadline. Asterisk launches campus by campus, free for students on a school email for the launch semester.

  • Lease Clinic. Photograph the lease, get the card, and add to your school's landlord index. The more students at your campus scan, the better the index for everyone after you.
