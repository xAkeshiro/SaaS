# Consumer AI landscape and white space (13 Sep 2026)

**Why this document exists.** The first recommendation (a campus attention platform) was rejected as too locked to educators. This pass looks only at universal, consumer or prosumer software that anyone can use, in the spirit of viral apps like Cluely. Two research agents ran ~170 searches; the sandbox proxy blocked most direct page fetches, so figures come from search-result extracts. ## 1. What Cluely actually teaches

Cluely's "cheat on everything" overlay got 70k signups in its first week (Apr 2025) and a $15M a16z round (Jun 2025). In March 2026 the CEO retracted the $7M ARR claim; the real figure was about $5.2M, and the product has been rebranded as an AI meeting note-taker ([TechCrunch, 5 Mar 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/05/cluely-ceo-roy-lee-admits-to-publicly-lying-about-revenue-numbers-last-year/)). Rage-bait bought attention; it did not buy retention, and the pivot landed in one of the most crowded categories in software (Granola alone is at a $1.5B valuation).

Monetization reality for AI apps (RevenueCat 2026, 115k apps): AI apps earn 41% more per user but churn 30–36% faster; twelve-month retention 21% vs 31% for non-AI apps; hard paywalls convert 10.7% vs 2.1% for freemium; weekly plans are now 55% of subscription revenue ([TechCrunch, Mar 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/10/ai-powered-apps-struggle-with-long-term-retention-new-report-shows)). Median price points: $7.48/week, $12.99/month, $38.42/year (Adapty 2026).

## 2. Where the giants now sit (do not build here)

- **OpenAI:** ChatGPT Health (Jan 2026, records import, visit-prep questions, Epic integration Sep 2026), Study Mode, group chats, proactive Pulse briefs, Instant Checkout walked back to discovery (Mar 2026), Sora killed.
- **Google:** Gemini Live camera and screen share, Personal Intelligence over Gmail, Photos, YouTube and Maps (global Apr 2026), Gmail AI Inbox, I/O 2026 Daily Brief and Universal Cart, Pixel Call Assist (Hold for Me, Direct My Call), scam and fake-call detection.
- **Apple:** iOS 26 Call Screening, Hold Assist, Live Translation, Mail summaries; WWDC 2026 Siri with personal context and on-screen awareness; iOS 27 lets users pick a third-party default assistant.
- **Meta:** Meta AI inside WhatsApp groups (~1B MAU); **Muse** personal agent launched 8 Sep 2026 at $0, $20 and $100 a month, handling emails, forms, travel and "negotiating bills" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/08/meta-debuts-its-muse-ai-agent-will-consumers-trust-it/)); acquired Limitless.
- **Amazon:** Alexa+ with proactive Daily Insights; owns Bee. **Samsung:** Now Nudge anticipatory suggestions. **Anthropic:** memory on all tiers; Cowork on web and mobile.

Sherlocked or saturated categories: general assistants, AI video feeds, ambient pendants, single-user health Q&A, generic tutoring, proactive daily briefs, call screening and hold, email triage and "slop" summarizing, group-chat AI participants (Meta AI, ChatGPT groups, plus Jarvie $8.3M, Continua $8M, Pally, Linq $20M, Series $5.1M), shopping and travel discovery, agentic browsers, doctor-visit recorders (VisitRecall, AlignCare, Kin Health $9M, plus ChatGPT Health and Epic's Emmie).

| AirHelp, WinIt | Flight compensation, NYC tickets | 35%, 50% success fees | Incumbents |
| Simple AI (YC), HeyRobyn, Assindo | General "make a call for me" apps | $15–70/month | Early, beta, few reviews |

Open inside this category, per the research: the post-visit **medical money stack** end to end (bill-vs-EOB reconciliation, coding audit, appeal, phone follow-up with billing offices and insurers, external review filing, deadline tracking) for the $200–5,000 long tail that Resolve ignores and Goodbill only partly covers. Evidence: fewer than 1% of denials are appealed while 44% of appeals succeed ([KFF](https://www.kff.org/patient-consumer-protections/claims-denials-and-appeals-in-aca-marketplace-plans-in-2024/)); 93% of people who negotiate a medical bill get relief ([LendingTree](https://press.lendingtree.com/news/press-releases/93-of-americans-who-negotiate-a-medical-bill-get-it-eliminated-or-reduced)); seven in ten Americans blame insurer denials at least partly for the 2024 UnitedHealthcare killing (NORC). It is a US-only, healthcare-shaped business, which is why it is a runner-up rather than the pick.

**Hard technical constraint for anything call-based:** third-party apps cannot access live cellular call audio on iOS or Android (Play banned the accessibility workaround in 2022; iOS never allowed it). An AI that calls for you must place calls over its own VoIP numbers, with the user bridged in for one-time codes or handoff. Unit cost of a 40-minute call with 30 minutes on hold: about $4 on a naive ElevenLabs stack, $2 on OpenAI Realtime, under $1.50 if hold runs on bare telephony with a music detector and the model joins only when a human answers. Since February 2024 the FCC treats AI voices as "artificial or prerecorded" under the TCPA; consumer-initiated calls to business lines are low risk, calling a landlord's cell is not. Maine, New York, Utah and California require chatbot disclosure. Pindrop synthetic-voice detection is now inside NICE and Zoom, so banks and insurers may flag AI callers as fraud.

## 5. Regulatory tailwinds around fine print and subscriptions

- FTC's click-to-cancel rule was vacated by the Eighth Circuit on 8 Jul 2025, leaving consumers without the federal rule; the FTC reopened rulemaking on 30 Jan 2026 ([Sidley](https://www.sidley.com/en/insights/newsupdates/2026/02/us-ftc-signals-renewed-interest-in-click-to-cancel-rulemaking)). State auto-renewal laws keep expanding (Connecticut click-to-cancel effective 1 Jul 2026; California, New York and others already in force).
- The FTC Junk Fees Rule for live-event tickets and lodging has been in effect since May 2025.
- Subscription waste: households spend $219 a month on subscriptions but estimate $86; 42% forgot at least one ([Self](https://www.self.inc/info/cost-of-unused-paid-subscriptions/)); 76% of adults say cancellation is made deliberately hard; an international sweep found dark patterns on 75.7% of 642 sites ([EmailTooltester](https://www.emailtooltester.com/en/blog/dark-patterns-canceling-subscription-report/)).
- The agent-era twist: Google's Universal Cart, OpenAI's checkout experiments and Meta Muse mean software will increasingly accept terms on people's behalf. A layer that reads and records what was agreed becomes more necessary, not less.

## 6. Investor-stated white space

- YC F26: "AI consumer products built for a billion people" across getting things done, money, health, social.
- a16z Big Ideas 2026: AI making expensive services cheap ("personal assistants, therapists, tutors"); "once-in-a-decade gold rush in consumer tech in 2026" via app platforms.
- Nate Jones, "The Anticipation Gap" (May 2026): no consumer agent has crossed from reactive to anticipatory; the unsolved problems are context, reliability, permission and judgment.
- Sequoia AI Ascent 2026: "sell the work, not the tool."
- Benchmark's Sarah Tavel (Aug 2026): the next wave is social, not solo; ChatGPT is "too complex for average users."
